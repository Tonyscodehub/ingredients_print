Label preview

Press a product to print its label. It will be shown here.

Check bought-in product

Check every word against the pack before accepting – online information can be wrong or out of date, and recipes change. The shop and manufacturer are never printed on the label.

Add bought-in product

Enter a barcode and press Look up (or Enter) to fill in the product name.

This adds a new column to the spreadsheet (a backup copy of the old file is kept), then opens the checking screen so you can check the ingredients against the pack. If the spreadsheet is open in Excel, close it first.

Settings
